Student Representative

One aspect of our mission as the Roosevelt High School Alumni Association is to support the current students of Roosevelt High School. In order to best do this, student voice is imperative.

The organization will be electing a rising junior to provide student perspective on our ongoing work. This student will serve a term of one school year.

Applicants must be a member of the Class of 2028 and have a minimum 3.0 GPA.

Applications are due on June 12th, 2026. The following application must be completed in entirety.

Applications will be reviewed at the June Association meeting, and all applicants will be notified of their status by June 30th.

Applications are now closed.

Student Representatives will be required to attend the three Board of Directors meetings during their term. These meetings are hosted in the RHS Library, and are discussion sessions for the RHSAA to talk about our wide variety of projects. They are held from 6:00-8:00pm on the following dates:
  • September 17th, 2026

  • January 21st, 2027

  • May 20th, 2027
If special circumstances arise close to one of these dates, we can discuss alternative plans.

At each Board of Directors meeting, we will allocate time on the agenda to hear from our Student Representatives. This will be a 5-10 minute opportunity for you to share news from the student body, such as recent accomplishments, upcoming events, or other items the Student Representative feels important to share with the organization.

On a monthly basis, Student Representatives will submit an upcoming event, activity, or project for alumni to attend or support. This information will be emailed to the Executive Committee by the 1st of each month to be shared with our wider alumni base.

Email the Executive Committee at executive@rooseveltalumni.org and we will get back to you!
