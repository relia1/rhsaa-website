Rose Festival Princess Steward

Time commitment 0-6 hours/month, with most activity concentrated in the spring during the Rose Festival season.

Participation will be primarily virtual, with in-person attendance at the Rose Festival Princess Coronation and related events as available.

We are seeking 1 Past Princess for this role.

Volunteer should have clear communication skills and comfort using email and basic computer tools.

Carry forward the shared history of RHS Rose Festival Princesses by maintaining connections across generations. Steward the relationships, records, and transitions that ensure this history remains intact and current over time. Through this work, each year’s princess is thoughtfully added to an ongoing historical record.

Duties & Responsibilities

- Coordinate communication with past princesses related to the annual Rose Festival Princess Coronation, including invitations and shared updates.
- Maintain contact information for past RHS Rose Festival Princesses and serve as the primary point of contact on behalf of the RHSAA.
- Serve as the primary liaison for the current year’s RHS Rose Festival Princess.
- Collect historical information, photos, and materials from the current princess for inclusion in the RHSAA’s records and displays.
- Coordinate with the History Committee and Communications Committee to ensure portraits, displays, and records related to the Rose Festival Princesses are updated.
- Attend any History Committee meetings or participate in Committee discussions.
   Most conversations will take place via email, with occasional meetings 2-3 times per year.
Collaborate closely with the History Committee, and work directly with past and current RHS Rose Festival Princesses as the primary point of coordination.

Interested in volunteering? Send an email to our Vice-President, Jim Kennison, at jim@rooseveltalumni.org with the subject line "Rose Festival Princess Steward" to learn more.
