Veterans Memorial Gardener

Time commitment 2 hours/month.

Participation will be in-person on campus.

We are seeking 2 volunteers for this role.

Volunteers should have an interest in gardening.

The Alumni Association maintains a Memorial Garden on campus   (learn more here)  dedicated to our alumni veterans and their families. This peaceful space is open to the public and serves as a place of remembrance and reflection. Volunteers help maintain the garden by keeping the area clean, cared for, and appropriately decorated so visitors can continue to experience it with respect.

Duties & Responsibilities

- Visit the Memorial Garden once per month to perform basic upkeep, including clearing debris, pulling weeds, trimming greenery, and wiping the memorial stones.
   This can be done at any time on the volunteer’s own schedule.
- Place flowers or decorations on special holidays such as Memorial Day.
- Communicate any maintenance needs to the Veterans Committee to support ongoing upkeep of the garden.

Interested in volunteering? Send an email to our Veterans Committee at veterans@rooseveltalumni.org with the subject line "Veterans Memorial Gardener" to learn more.
