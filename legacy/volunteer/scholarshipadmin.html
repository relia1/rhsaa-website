Scholarship Application Administrator

Time commitment is 6 hours/week during the application window (February through April).

Participation will primarily be virtual and over email.

We are seeking 1 volunteer for this role.

Volunteer must be comfortable using email and Google Workspace tools, have a strong attention to detail, and the ability to work responsibly with sensitive information.

Support a fair and consistent scholarship process by managing applications from submission through review. Ensure eligibility is verified, student information is handled responsibly, and applications are prepared for blind evaluation by the Scholarship Committee.

Duties & Responsibilities

- Set up and update the scholarship application Google Form each year in coordination with the Scholarship Committee and Committee Chair.
- Monitor incoming scholarship applications during the application window and review submissions within 72 hours to confirm eligibility based on defined criteria.
- Communicate with students via email to confirm receipt and eligibility, or to request corrections to application materials prior to the deadline when needed.
- Process completed applications after the deadline by removing personally identifying information and preparing anonymized materials for distribution to voting reviewers on the Scholarship Committee.
- Track and compile basic applicant statistics for use in future reporting and planning.
- Attend key Scholarship Committee meetings related to the application cycle, including the planning meeting prior to the application opening and the retrospective meeting after awards are finalized.
   These meetings may take place via email or as a hybrid meeting with in-person and virtual options.
Collaborate with the Scholarship Committee at-large.

Interested in volunteering? Send an email to our Scholarship Committee at scholarships@rooseveltalumni.org with the subject line "Scholarship Application Administrator" to learn more.
We can't wait to have you join us!
