Social Media Graphic Designer

Time commitment 10 hours/month.

Participation will be virtual, business conducted over email and instant messaging.

We are seeking 2 volunteers for this role.

Volunteers should have experience using Canva, or experience with a similar design tool and a willingness to learn Canva, along with a creative eye and strong attention to detail.

Create graphics used across the Alumni Association’s social media channels to accompany updates, announcements, and other shared content. This work helps shape how the alumni community sees and connects with what’s happening at Roosevelt and our Association.

Duties & Responsibilities

- Design social media graphics in Canva using provided information, following established brand guidelines and existing visual styles.
   Posting and scheduling of content are handled by another role.
- Review graphics created by other volunteers and provide feedback or suggested revisions.
- Attend any Communications Committee meetings or participate in Committee discussions.
   Meetings will likely be in the form of email and only occur when special topics arise.
Collaborate with the Communications Committee at large. Receive information from the Chair, and discuss graphics with other designers.

Interested in volunteering? Send an email to our Communications Chair, Megan Hall, at megan@rooseveltalumni.org with the subject line "Social Media Graphic Designer" to learn more.
We can't wait to have you join us!
