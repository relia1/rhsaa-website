Newspaper Liaison

Time commitment 0-6 hours/month, depending on media needs.

Participation will be virtual, with communication conducted primarily over email and occasional phone calls.

We are seeking 1 volunteer for this role.

Volunteers should have strong written communication skills and be comfortable communicating on behalf of the organization.

Act as a consistent voice for the Alumni Association when working with local newspapers. This work centers on collaboration and coordination, helping connect the right people and information so updates are shared accurately.

Duties & Responsibilities

- Serve as the primary point of contact between the Alumni Association and local newspapers.
- Respond to inquiries from newspapers by answering questions, sharing information, or directing requests to the appropriate committee or board member.
- Share updates, announcements, or context with newspapers as appropriate, in coordination with the Communications Committee.
Attend any Communications Committee meetings or participate in Committee discussions.
   Meetings will likely be in the form of email and only occur when special topics arise.
Work closely with the Communications Committee, with occasional coordination with the Executive Committee and other RHSAA committees as needed.

Interested in volunteering? Send an email to our Communications Chair, Megan Hall, at megan@rooseveltalumni.org with the subject line "Newspaper Liaison" to learn more.
We can't wait to have you join us!
