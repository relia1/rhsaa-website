Student Representative Mentor

Time commitment 3-6 hours/month.

Participation will be primarily virtual, with most communication via email, and in-person attendance at board meetings.

We are seeking 1 volunteer for this role.

Volunteers should have experience working with youth in a structured or supervised setting, and comfort using email and related tools.

Student Representatives play an important role in connecting current students with the Alumni Association and sharing student perspectives with the Board. Having a consistent, trusted point of contact helps ensure those students feel supported, informed, and comfortable participating throughout the year. This role provides continuity and guidance so Student Representatives can engage confidently, communicate clearly, and focus on representing their peers effectively.

Duties & Responsibilities

- Serve as the primary point of contact for the Student Representatives, acting as a consistent channel for communication between the students and the RHSAA Board.
- Receive monthly reports from the Student Representatives and follow up with questions as needed.
- Support Student Representatives in preparing for their presentations at RHSAA board meetings throughout the year.
- Connect with new Student Representatives at the start of each year to share information about expectations, timelines, and how their role works.
- (Optional) Attend Executive Committee meetings held on even-numbered months to support discussion of Student Representative topics.
Work with committee leads across the organization to help source volunteers for short-term needs. Coordinate with the Communications Committee when broader outreach is needed.

Interested in volunteering? Send an email to our Executive Committee at executive@rooseveltalumni.org with the subject line "Student Representative Mentor" to learn more.
We can't wait to have you join us!
