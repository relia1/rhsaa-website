Digital History Designer

Time commitment 2-6 hours/month, depending on projects.

Participation will be primarily virtual, with design work completed remotely.

We are seeking 2 volunteers for this role.

Volunteers should have experience using Canva, or experience with a similar design tool and a willingness to learn Canva, along with a creative eye and strong attention to detail.

The History Committee works to preserve and share Roosevelt’s story in ways that feel engaging and accessible to students, alumni, and the broader community. Creative volunteers help shape how that history is seen and experienced, turning years of memories and achievements into visuals that connect people across generations. This role offers a chance to contribute creatively while helping keep Roosevelt’s legacy visible and meaningful.

Duties & Responsibilities

- Design and update digital history displays, such as slideshows used for on-campus screens or presentations.
- Create visual layouts or graphics to support History Committee projects, including posters, digital displays, or similar materials.
- Collaborate with History Committee members to gather content and adjust designs as projects evolve.
- Attend any History Committee meetings or participate in Committee discussions.
   Most conversations will take place via email, with occasional meetings 2-3 times per year.
Collaborate closely with the History Committee at-large.

Interested in volunteering? Send an email to our History Committee at history@rooseveltalumni.org with the subject line "Digital History Designer" to learn more.
We can't wait to have you join us!
