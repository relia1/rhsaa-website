Social Media Assistant

Time commitment 6 hours/month.

Participation will primarily virtual and over email or instant messaging.

We are seeking 2 volunteers for this role.

Volunteers should have an interest in communications, and prior experience with social media for personal use.

Social media helps the RHSAA stay connected with alumni by sharing stories, history, and updates from across the Roosevelt community. Many alumni enjoy seeing reminders of their time at school, learning about ongoing projects, and finding ways to stay involved. Helping maintain a steady and engaging social media presence supports that connection and keeps alumni informed about the Association’s work.

Duties & Responsibilities

- Help draft and schedule posts for RHSAA’s Facebook and Instagram accounts that share news, events, and updates.
- Monitor engagement on social media posts and respond or flag comments and messages as appropriate.
- Participate in the development of strategies to reach new alumni audience members and continue to grow our reach.
- Attend any Communications Committee meetings or participate in Committee discussions.
   Meetings will likely be in the form of email and only occur when special topics arise.

Interested in volunteering? Send an email to our Communications Chair at megan@rooseveltalumni.org with the subject line "Social Media Assistant" to learn more.
We can't wait to have you join us!
