Staff Liaison

Time commitment 2-3 hours/month.

Participation will primarily be virtual via email, with attendance at board meetings on campus.

We are seeking 1 volunteer for this role.

Volunteer must be an alumnus who is currently a Roosevelt High School staff member. Volunteer should be familiar with the current Roosevelt staff community and able to provide connections and thoughtful perspective when needed.

As an alumnus who now serves as a Roosevelt staff member, this position holds a unique perspective shaped by both lived student experience and a current understanding of the school environment. That dual role helps bridge conversations between RHSAA and the day-to-day realities of supporting students and staff. By sharing insight and helping strengthen connections, this presence helps RHSAA better understand how to support the school community as it continues its mission of serving Roughriders.

Duties & Responsibilities

- Attend RHSAA board meetings three times per year and share general updates or perspective from a staff point of view when appropriate.
- Serve as a staff-connected perspective for RHSAA, offering insight and helping connect leadership with relevant staff or programs when needed.
Collaborate with the RHSAA Board of Directors and support connections to Roosevelt staff or school programs as appropriate.

Interested in volunteering? Send an email to our Executive Committee at executive@rooseveltalumni.org with the subject line "Staff Liaison" to learn more.
We can't wait to have you join us!
