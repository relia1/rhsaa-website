Athletic Hall of Fame Data Coordinator

Time commitment 4-6 hours/month.

Participation will primarily be virtual, with occasional in-person committee meetings as available.

We are seeking 1 volunteer for this role.

Volunteers should be comfortable working in Google Drive and spreadsheets, have strong organizational skills, attention to detail, and an interest in athletics.

Support the Athletic Hall of Fame Committee by helping organize and maintain records of past and prospective nominees. Working alongside committee researchers, careful tracking of eligibility, research, and nomination materials keeps information structured and accessible over time. Together, this work helps ensure significant athletic achievements are recognized and notable alumni are honored.

Duties & Responsibilities

- Maintain and update master tracking spreadsheets for eligible and prospective Athletic Hall of Fame nominees.
- Collect and organize research provided by committee members for easy reference and long-term tracking.
- Draft nomination forms for selected nominees and revise drafts in collaboration with the Committee.
- (Optional) Attend the Induction Banquets in the fall to celebrate the new inductees.
- Attend any Athletic Hall of Fame Committee meetings or participate in Committee discussions.
   Most conversations will take place via email, with occasional in-person meetings as available.
Collaborate closely with the Athletic Hall of Fame Committee. Occasionally coordinate with the History Committee to ensure records remain consistent across projects and share finalized nominee information with the Communications Committee as inductees are announced.

Interested in volunteering? Send an email to our Athletic Hall of Fame Committee spokesperson, Jim Kennison, at jim@rooseveltalumni.org with the subject line "Athletic Hall of Fame Data Coordinator" to learn more.
