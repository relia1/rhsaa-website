Volunteer Coordinator

Time commitment 4-6 hours/month, depending upon seasonal projects.

Participation will be hybrid, with most communication via email and phone, and occasional in-person coordination as needed.

We are seeking 1 volunteer for this role.

Volunteers should be comfortable using email, phone, and Google Workspace as part of coordinating volunteer requests.

Many RHSAA projects and events benefit from extra help at specific moments, rather than long-term volunteer commitments. Having a central point of contact makes it easier for committees to find support when they need it and for alumni to step in for short-term opportunities that fit their availability. This role helps connect people with meaningful ways to contribute while keeping volunteer involvement flexible, focused, and approachable.

Duties & Responsibilities

- Serve as a central point of contact for committees seeking short-term or one-off volunteer help.
- Reach out to past or potential volunteers to source support for specific needs, and connect volunteers with the appropriate project or committee leads.
- Maintain a simple record of volunteer contacts, interests, and past involvement for future reference.
- Coordinate with the Communications Committee to share volunteer needs publicly when broader outreach is helpful.
- Attend Executive Committee meetings held on even-numbered months to support discussion of volunteer needs.
Work with committee leads across the organization to help source volunteers for short-term needs. Coordinate with the Communications Committee when broader outreach is needed.

Interested in volunteering? Send an email to our Executive Committee at executive@rooseveltalumni.org with the subject line "Volunteer Coordinator" to learn more.
We can't wait to have you join us!
