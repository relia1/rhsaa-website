Newsletter Contributor

Time commitment 6 hours/month.

Participation will be virtual and over email.

We are seeking 3 volunteers for this role.

Volunteers should have an interest in writing, and be comfortable using Word or Google Docs.

Newsletter Contributors help write sections of the monthly email newsletter using provided information. This role is ideal for volunteers who enjoy writing and want a flexible, behind-the-scenes way to contribute to our alumni community on a monthly basis.

Duties & Responsibilities

- Prepare short newsletter blurbs from provided information within a one-week turnaround each month.
- Revise written content as requested based on reviewer feedback.
- Attend any Communications Committee meetings or participate in Committee discussions.
   Meetings will likely be in the form of email and only occur when special topics arise.
Collaborate with the Communications Committee at large. Receive information from the Chair, and discuss newsletter content with other writers and reviewers.

Interested in volunteering? Send an email to our Communications Chair, Megan Hall, at megan@rooseveltalumni.org with the subject line "Newsletter Contributor" to learn more.
We can't wait to have you join us!
