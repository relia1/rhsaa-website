Picnic Day-Of Coordinator

Time commitment of 1–2 hours/month, April through July. Full-day at the All-Class Picnic.

Participation will be a hybrid of virtual and in-person, planning and coordination primarily take place over email, and attendance at the All-Class Picnic in July.

We are seeking 1 volunteer for this role.

Volunteers should be able to stay organized and calm while guiding a busy event environment. Comfortable making real-time decisions and directing volunteers as needed.

The All-Class Picnic is one of RHSAA’s most established traditions, built on simple planning and a welcoming atmosphere. A day-of coordinator helps keep things moving by serving as a central point of contact, guiding volunteers and supporting the flow of the event. With a clear plan already in place, this role focuses on steady oversight rather than heavy planning, making the day feel smooth and low-stress for everyone involved.

Duties & Responsibilities

- Participate in Picnic Committee planning conversations beginning in early spring, primarily via email and monthly in-person meetings starting in April.
- On the day of the All-Class Picnic, serve as the main on-site point of contact, helping guide setup, event flow, and cleanup based on the established plan.
- Direct volunteers and answer day-of questions, ensuring people know where to go and what to focus on.
- Coordinate with park staff or partners on-site if questions arise.
- Make small, practical decisions throughout the day to help keep the event running smoothly.
Collaborate with the Picnic Committee at large to support day-of coordination.

Interested in volunteering? Send an email to our Picnic Committee at events@rooseveltalumni.org with the subject line "Picnic Day-Of Coordinator" to learn more.
