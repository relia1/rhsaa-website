Decade Representative

Time commitment 2-4 hours/month.

Participation will be virtual, with most communication via email.

We are seeking 1 volunteer per decade for this role.

Volunteers should be comfortable communicating over email.

We are still seeking representatives for the following decades: 1950s, 1960s, 1970s, 1980s, 1990s, 2000s, 2010s, 2020s.
Every graduating class is part of Roosevelt’s story, and this role helps keep your decade connected to that shared history. Decade Representatives help make sure alumni from their generation stay part of the conversation, from reunions to the ongoing life of the school and Alumni Association. As a member of the decade yourself, you also bring perspective from that generation of Roosevelt alumni and help ensure your classmates remain part of the Roosevelt community.

Duties & Responsibilities

- Maintain a contact for each graduating class within the assigned decade (ex. 1990 - 1999).
- Reach out to classes hosting milestone reunions to introduce the Alumni Association, offer support, and gather information that can be shared with the community.
- Share relevant RHSAA updates with class contacts and pass along alumni news, updates, or reunion information when appropriate.
- Attend any Communications Committee meetings or participate in Committee discussions.
   Meetings will likely be in the form of email and only occur when special topics arise.
Collaborate with the Communications Committee and maintain relationships with alumni contacts representing classes within the assigned decade.

Interested in volunteering? Send an email to our Communications Chair, Megan Hall, at megan@rooseveltalumni.org with the subject line "Decade Representative" to learn more.
We can't wait to have you join us!
