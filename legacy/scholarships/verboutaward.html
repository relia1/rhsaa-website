Mike Verbout Roughrider Award

The Roosevelt High School Alumni Association is proud to announce the creation of the Mike Verbout Roughrider Award, established to honor the life and leadership of our longtime president, Mike Verbout ’62, who passed away in February 2026.

This perpetaul annual award will recognize a student who embodies the ideals Mike championed throughout his life—Roughrider pride, service to community, and unwavering advocacy for Roosevelt High School. This year’s inaugural award included a $1,000 college scholarship.

A proud graduate of Roosevelt High School (Class of 1962) and Lewis & Clark College, Mike dedicated his career to education, serving as principal of Applegate, Beach, and James John elementary schools. As the founder and president of the Roosevelt Alumni Association for more than 15 years, he helped define and shape our mission, leaving a legacy that continues to guide our work today.

The award ensures that Mike’s spirit of leadership and loyalty will continue to inspire future generations of Roughriders.

2026


Hazel Patterson

Class of 2026

Hazel Patterson is the inaugural recipient of the Mike Verbout Roughrider Award. This annual award honors the life and leadership of the Roosevelt Alumni Association founder and long time President. Hazel is an accomplished member of the Roosevelt High School Class of 2026, recognized for outstanding academic achievement, leadership, and community service. Graduating with a 4.19 GPA, Hazel has been honored as an AP Scholar with Honor, received the Exhibitionist Award from the Portland Art Museum, and earned the School Spirit Award for exceptional dedication to school culture.

A passionate advocate for civic engagement, her leadership is evident through service as a Student Council Representative, Student Representative to the Roosevelt Alumni Association, and Co-President of the Philosophy & Discussion Club. As Team Captain for Roosevelt's Cross Country team and a Varsity Track & Field athlete, Hazel has demonstrated commitment, teamwork, and perseverance.

Hazel is the co-founder of The Giving Tree Program, a seasonal charity that has provided hundreds of gifts to underprivileged students across North Portland schools. This initiative, which nearly doubled its reach in one year, demonstrates dedication to making a tangible impact in the community. She also volunteers at Assumption Village, supporting residents through activities and community events.

In addition to community service, Hazel has professional experience as a Customer Service Specialist at Dick’s Sporting Goods, where mentoring new employees and managing key departments have further developed communication and leadership skills.

Hazel’s journey is defined by resilience, collaboration, and a commitment to service—qualities that will continue to drive future success in her higher education pursuits at George Washington University.
