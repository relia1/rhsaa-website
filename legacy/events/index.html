2026 Homecoming

Friday, October 16th, 2026
Rider Stadium, Roosevelt High School

Join us for a community celebration of Roosevelt High School!
Free Community BBQ: We'll be serving meals from 4:00pm up to game time, bring your family and grab a dish. Find us in the parking lot turn-around on the west side of campus. Hosted by the Alumni Association and the Roosevelt Supporter Club.
Tailgate Activities: Check out games, booths, and other activities. Located on the softball field and into the parking lot. Hosted by student leadership.
Football vs. Cleveland: Pack the stands for what is sure to be a great game, padded with performances by Band, Cheer, and Dance. Enter through the gate in the parking lot.
Football tickets are $7 for adults, $5 for students and senior citizens. Concessions will be available for purchase, funds support a variety of student clubs.
