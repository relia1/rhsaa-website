Grant Recipients

One aspect of our mission as the Roosevelt High School Alumni Association is to support the students and staff of Roosevelt High School. Part of how we do that is by providing grants towards projects that students are passionate about.

Below are the grants awarded by the RHSAA, organized by fiscal year. Each grant includes the amount, purpose, and photos.
Support the project:

We award grants on revolving basis, and there are always more needs to fund. If you would like to make a donation to support this initiative, please visit our donation page and note "Student Grants" on your check or PayPal contribution.
